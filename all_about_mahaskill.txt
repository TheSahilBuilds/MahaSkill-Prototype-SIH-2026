1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MahaSkill AI is an AI-powered skill intelligence, career guidance, and longitudinal
outcome tracking platform designed specifically for the state of Maharashtra under SIH 2026. 

Vision:
"Build a skilled, employable, and future-ready workforce in Maharashtra."

Mission:
"Bridge the gap between student competencies, academic curricula, and real-time
industry requirements across Maharashtra's industrial and technology clusters through
intelligent skill analysis, personalized learning roadmaps, and longitudinal outcome tracking."

Key Goals:
- Trainee-First Continuous Journey: Guided workflow from skill gap diagnosis to job outcome tracking.
- Track post-training outcomes: Employment, self-employment, apprenticeships, wage growth, and retention.
- Provide trainees with instant clarity on skill gaps and personalized upskilling roadmaps.
- Execute automated/assisted follow-ups at 30, 90, 180 days and 12 months post-training.
- Empower government administrators with summary-first, evidence-based policy analytics via `/outcome-dashboard`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & BACKGROUND (SIH 2026 SOURCE OF TRUTH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why MahaSkill AI was Created:
1. Incomplete Post-Training Outcome Data:
   Training portals track enrolment, attendance, assessment, and certification, but reliable data
   on employment, self-employment, wage growth, retention, and training relevance is missing.

2. Industry-Academia Disconnect:
   Academic courses often lag behind rapidly evolving tech stack demands (e.g., practical SQL/DAX,
   Cloud deployment, Python analytics). Students graduate without knowing what skills employers require.

3. Lack of Regional & Demographic Skill Intelligence:
   Students in Tier-2/Tier-3 districts in Maharashtra lack visibility into salary ranges, hiring
   patterns, and high-demand skills in major tech hubs like Pune and Mumbai.

3. TARGET AUDIENCE & USER ROLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MahaSkill AI serves dual user roles:

1. Trainees / Students (Primary User Journey):
   - Continuous, low-burden workflow:
     Home → Check Skill Gap → Login & Consent → 7-Step Survey → Skill Gap Analysis →
     Recommended Courses → Upskilling Roadmap → My Training → Progress & Completion →
     Sample Certificate → Outcome Follow-up → My Outcome Dashboard (`/my-dashboard`).
   - Personal outcome dashboard displaying employment signal, TCS placement, +39.1% wage growth,
     and retention timeline.

2. Government Administrators & Policy Makers (Internal Route `/outcome-dashboard`):
   - Summary-First analytical dashboard focused on state-wide KPIs, non-placement barriers,
     6-month retention metrics, wage growth, district benchmarks, and provider performance.

4. CORE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Technology Stack:
- Frontend Core: React 19 (JSX) with Vite 8
- Routing: React Router DOM v7
- Data Visualization: Recharts 3 & Tailwind metric cards
- Icons: Lucide React (`lucide-react`)
- Styling: TailwindCSS v4 with official Maharashtra Government color aesthetic:
  - Deep Navy Blue (`#062B52`) & Primary Blue (`#0B3B70`)
  - Accent Gold (`#F2A900`) & Light Background (`#F5F7FA`)
- State Management: React Context API (`AppContext.jsx`) with LocalStorage persistence:
  - `mahaSkillCourses`, `mahaSkillSurvey`, `mahaSkillAnalysis`, `mahaSkillOutcome`, `mahaSkillConsent`, `mahaSkillFollowup`.
- Internationalization (i18n): Dual-language engine (English & Marathi `mr`).
- Data Credibility Labeling: All data explicitly tagged `"Prototype Dataset • Illustrative Data"`, `"Demo Verification"`, or `"Prototype / Sample Certificate"`.

[F] Interactive Learning Roadmap (SkillRoadmap.jsx & roadmaps.js)
