1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MahaSkill AI is a skill intelligence, career guidance, and longitudinal outcome
tracking platform designed specifically for the state of Maharashtra under SIH 2026.

Primary Messaging:
"From Skill Development to Real Outcomes"

Supporting Text:
"Identify skill gaps, build the right skills, and track what happens after training — from employment and retention to wage progression."

Primary Product Flow — Source of Truth:
HOME 
  ↓
CHECK MY SKILL GAP 
  ↓
10-QUESTION SURVEY (4 Steps)
  ↓
SKILL ANALYSIS 
  ↓
RECOMMENDED COURSES 
  ↓
PERSONALIZED ROADMAP 
  ↓
MY TRAINING (3 Tabs: Courses, Follow-ups, Certificates)
  ↓
COURSE COMPLETION 
  ↓
SAMPLE CERTIFICATE 
  ↓
OUTCOME FOLLOW-UP (30D, 90D, 180D, 365D)
  ↓
MY OUTCOME DASHBOARD (/my-dashboard)

Key Goals:
- Trainee-First Continuous Journey: Guided workflow from skill gap diagnosis to job outcome tracking.
- Track post-training outcomes: Employment, self-employment, apprenticeships, wage growth, retention, and training relevance.
- Provide trainees with instant clarity on skill gaps and personalized upskilling roadmaps.
- Execute automated follow-ups at 30, 90, 180 days and 12 months post-training.
- Empower government administrators with summary-first, evidence-based policy analytics via `/outcome-dashboard`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & BACKGROUND (SIH 2026 SOURCE OF TRUTH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why MahaSkill AI was Created:
"Training systems often know that a trainee enrolled, attended, was assessed and received a certificate, but may not know what happened afterward — whether the trainee got a job, became self-employed, retained the job, improved wages, or found the training relevant."

MahaSkill's Solution Framework:
BEFORE TRAINING
  Skill Gap → Recommended Course → Roadmap

DURING TRAINING
  Training Progress → Completion → Certificate

AFTER TRAINING
  Outcome Follow-up → Employment → Retention → Wage Progression → Training Relevance

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TARGET AUDIENCE & USER ROLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MahaSkill AI serves dual user roles:

1. Trainees / Students (Primary User Journey):
   - Continuous, low-burden workflow:
     Home → Check Skill Gap → 10-Question Survey → Skill Gap Analysis →
     Recommended Courses → Upskilling Roadmap → My Training → Progress & Completion →
     Sample Certificate → Outcome Follow-up → My Outcome Dashboard (`/my-dashboard`).
   - Personal outcome dashboard displaying employment signal, TCS placement, +39.1% wage growth,
     6-month retention milestone, and training relevance.

2. Government Administrators & Policy Makers (Internal Route `/outcome-dashboard`):
   - Summary-First analytical dashboard focused on state-wide KPIs, non-placement barriers,
     6-month retention metrics, wage growth, district benchmarks, and provider performance.

4. CORE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Technology Stack:
- Frontend Core: React 19 (JSX) with Vite 8
- Routing: React Router DOM v7
- Data Visualization: Recharts 3 & Tailwind metric cards
- Icons: Lucide React (`lucide-react`)
- Styling: TailwindCSS v4 with official Maharashtra Government color aesthetic:
  - Deep Navy Blue (`#062B52`) & Primary Blue (`#0B3B70`)
  - Accent Gold (`#F2A900`) & Light Background (`#F5F7FA`)
- State Management: React Context API (`AppContext.jsx`) with LocalStorage persistence:
  - `mahaSkillCourses`, `mahaSkillSurvey`, `mahaSkillAnalysis`, `mahaSkillOutcome`, `mahaSkillConsent`, `mahaSkillFollowup`.
- Data Credibility Labeling: All data explicitly tagged `"Prototype Dataset • Illustrative Demo Data"`, `"Demo Verification"`, or `"Prototype / Sample Certificate"`.
